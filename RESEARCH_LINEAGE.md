# Research Lineage

Portable Rights is part of an evolving research lineage within
[SSTM Research](https://github.com/sstm-research).

This document preserves the relationship between the original research,
the independent implementation developed during the hackathon, and the
research questions that emerged from that implementation.

---

## 01 — Portable Trust

**Type:** Original research  
**Repository:** https://github.com/sstm-research/portable_trust

Portable Trust investigated how creators and physical artworks could
carry verifiable evidence across organizations and platforms.

The research proposed an artwork lifecycle:

**CREATE → AUTHENTICATE → EXHIBIT → SELL → TRANSFER → RESELL → LICENSE → REPRODUCE**

Its central research question was:

> Can a physical artwork carry a verifiable lifecycle of authorship,
> provenance, transfers and rights, with claims issued by independent
> actors, without depending on a single platform as the authority?

For the EAG Global Buildathon in Florianópolis, the research proposed
a practical challenge around authenticity, ownership transfer and
creator resale rights.

## 02 — Legallery

**Type:** Independent hackathon implementation

**SSTM Research fork:**  
https://github.com/sstm-research/legallery

**Original repository:**  
https://github.com/Gowdakthegreat/legallery

**Project submission:**  
https://devfolio.co/projects/legallery-2a79

During the EAG Global Buildathon, the participating team implemented
a concrete application informed by the Portable Trust research.

Legallery explored the relationship between artwork transactions and
Brazil's Artist's Resale Right (*direito de sequência*).

### Result

**2nd place — EAG track**

Each participating team member received a **USD 500 prize**.

The SSTM Research repository is intentionally preserved as a GitHub fork.

This keeps the original repository, authorship, contributors and commit
history connected to the implementation.

## 03 — Portable Rights

**Type:** Research evolution  
**Repository:** https://github.com/sstm-research/portable_rights

Portable Rights begins after the Legallery experiment.

It asks what happens when Portable Trust moves from making evidence
portable to making creator-rights events understandable and actionable.

The research focuses on:

**AUTHORSHIP**  
↓  
**ARTWORK IDENTITY**  
↓  
**PROVENANCE**  
↓  
**TRANSFER**  
↓  
**RESALE EVENT**  
↓  
**CREATOR RIGHTS**  
↓  
**RIGHTS INFRASTRUCTURE**

Portable Rights does not replace Portable Trust or Legallery.

Each artifact preserves a different moment in the research:

**Portable Trust**  
Research hypothesis and trust architecture.

↓

**Legallery**  
Independent implementation and field validation.

↓

**Portable Rights**  
Research synthesis after the implementation.
