## Portable Trust

**Repository:**  
https://github.com/sstm-research/portable_trust

Portable Trust is the research foundation that preceded the Legallery
implementation.

It proposed the research challenge around:

- authorship
- authenticity
- artwork provenance
- ownership transfer
- resale events
- creator rights
- interoperable trust infrastructure

Research authorship remains with the author and contributors identified
in the Portable Trust repository and publication.

## Portable Rights

**Repository:**  
https://github.com/sstm-research/portable_rights

Portable Rights is a later research synthesis created after the Legallery
implementation.

It investigates the consequences of portable lifecycle evidence for:

- creator rights
- resale events
- artwork provenance
- rights infrastructure
- institutional interoperability

Portable Rights should therefore be understood as a research evolution,
rather than as a claim of authorship over the Legallery software.

## Institutional and field contributions

Portable Trust and Portable Rights have also been informed by dialogue,
standards, organizations and research contexts documented in the
underlying research materials.

Where an institution, organization, project or individual contributed
knowledge, validation, implementation, infrastructure or field evidence,
that contribution should be attributed according to its actual role.

Reference does not imply endorsement, partnership or co-authorship
unless explicitly documented.

---

## Attribution principle

Research authorship, software authorship and institutional contribution
are different forms of participation.

A contributor to one experiment does not automatically become an author
of every later research artifact.

Likewise, inclusion of third-party work in SSTM Research does not transfer
its ownership or authorship.

Original repositories, commits, licenses and credits should remain
visible whenever possible.
