# Portable Rights

**From Portable Trust to actionable creator rights**

Portable Rights is a research evolution of
[Portable Trust](https://github.com/sstm-research/portable_trust).

It investigates how portable evidence about authorship, provenance,
ownership transfers, resale events and creator rights can become
actionable across independent platforms and institutions.

## Research lineage

Portable Trust established the research foundation.

At the EAG Global Buildathon in Florianópolis, part of this research
was implemented by the hackathon team as **Legallery**.

↓  
Legallery  
↓  
Portable Rights

## Relationship to Portable Trust

**Portable Trust** is the research foundation.

**Legallery** is an independent implementation of part of that research.

**Portable Rights** is the research layer created after the implementation,
focused specifically on the economic and rights consequences of portable
lifecycle evidence.

## Research principles

### Lastro

Who issued the information, under which authority, and with which evidence?

### Rastro

What happened to the artwork, when, between which actors, and how can
that event be verified?

### Infrastructure

How can identity, provenance and rights evidence travel between creators,
collectors, galleries, rights organizations and public infrastructure?
